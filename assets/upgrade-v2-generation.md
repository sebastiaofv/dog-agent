## Dusk city prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: finished background art for a secret-agent dog arcade game.
Input image: reference only for polished cartoon environment rendering, not a layout to preserve.
Primary request: An atmospheric fictional European city at blue hour, viewed from a playable rooftop terrace. A premium animated-film game environment, detailed soft shaded illustration, warm amber windows, muted teal and navy buildings, peach sunset near the horizon and indigo upper sky. No characters.
Composition: wide landscape canvas. The horizon and dense layered city occupy the middle of the scene, beginning around 28% from the top, so a narrow central portrait crop still has interesting architecture instead of empty sky. A clear flat rooftop floor occupies the bottom 23%, with a low stone parapet just behind it; the center foreground is unobstructed for the player. Framing buildings at the far edges, soft distant silhouettes, a subtle warm-lit bank-like building with columns in the distance, no readable signs. Keep the center readable and gently low contrast behind falling gold files. Cinematic but friendly, not gritty or scary.
Constraints: background only, no dog, people, vehicles in foreground, logos, UI, lettering or watermark. Fully painted to all edges, no transparency. Match the tactile soft shading of the reference while creating a distinct dusk scene.
